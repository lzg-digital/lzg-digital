Local SEO & MEO

Two different searches, both worth winning.

Local SEO covers how your business ranks in regular Google search results for people nearby. MEO — Map Engine Optimization — covers how you appear inside Google Maps and the Local Pack, where your Google Business Profile, reviews and location signals matter most. Most local businesses need both, not one or the other.

FAQ

A few quick answers.

Do you guarantee Google rankings?

No. Search results depend on many factors outside any agency's control. We focus on sustainable improvements and transparent reporting.

What is the difference between Local SEO and MEO?

Local SEO covers your ranking in standard search results; MEO covers your visibility inside Google Maps and the Local Pack. Most businesses benefit from both.

Do I need a full website, or is a Google Business Profile enough?

It depends on the business. We assess your situation and recommend the simplest setup that supports your goals — not the biggest package available.
