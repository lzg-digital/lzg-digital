Privacy Policy

This policy explains how LZG Digital handles information when you visit this website or contact us about our services.

Last updated: 6 September 2026

1. Information we may receive

We may receive information you choose to provide when you contact LZG Digital, such as your name, email address, phone number, business details, project requirements, or other information included in your message.

2. How we use information

We use information you provide to respond to enquiries, discuss projects, deliver requested services, provide support, and communicate with you about a current or potential business relationship.

3. Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, or other non-essential tracking technologies. We also do not currently use a cookie consent banner because there are no non-essential cookies being set by the site.

4. Third-party services

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit the site, the hosting infrastructure may process technical information needed to deliver web pages and maintain security. If you follow links to third-party services such as Google, WhatsApp, Instagram, or other external websites, those services have their own privacy practices and policies.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share information where reasonably necessary to provide a requested service, operate the website, comply with a legal obligation, or protect our rights and security.

6. Data retention

We keep enquiry and project information only for as long as reasonably necessary for communication, service delivery, record-keeping, or legal obligations.

7. Your choices

You may contact us to ask what personal information we hold about your interactions with us or to request correction or deletion where applicable.
